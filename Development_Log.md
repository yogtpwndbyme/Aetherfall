# Project Vision

Aetherfall is a hero-based fantasy competitive game inspired by the feeling of Warcraft 3 heroes and custom maps.

The goal is not to create another MOBA.

The goal is to create a new type of fantasy PvPvE experience where players control powerful heroes, explore a large world, level up, find items, defeat monsters, kill bosses and fight other players.

The game should combine:

- Warcraft 3 hero gameplay
- RPG progression
- PvP combat
- PvE exploration
- Boss encounters
- Random loot
- Large living maps

The most important goal:

Every match should create memorable stories.

Examples:

A team barely defeats a legendary boss and another team arrives to steal the reward.

A player finds an extremely rare item and becomes a threat.

A weaker team defeats stronger players through better teamwork and skill.

A group escapes with legendary loot while being hunted by enemies.

# Why Small Teams Instead Of Huge Armies

Huge teams can make individual players feel insignificant.

Aetherfall should make every player feel like their hero matters.

The player should think:

"My hero became powerful."

"My team defeated that boss."

"My decision changed the fight."

The world can still contain many players, but the main social unit should be a small party.

# Boss System

Bosses are a major part of Aetherfall.

Bosses should not just be stronger creeps.

They should feel like MMO raid encounters.

Bosses require:

- Teamwork
- Positioning
- Skill
- Timing
- Communication

Players should not be able to simply overpower bosses with stats.

A skilled team should beat a stronger team if they understand the mechanics.

# World Boss Idea

After some time in a match, a huge boss can spawn.

Example:

"The Worldbreaker has awakened."

The boss is so difficult that one player cannot kill it.

Even one small team may struggle.

Multiple teams may need to cooperate temporarily.

However:

When the boss is almost dead, teams may fight each other for the reward.

This creates unpredictable moments.

# Stat Upgrade System

Blacksmiths exist on maps.

Approximately:

4 blacksmith locations.

Mainly for endgame.

Players fight over access.

Upgrades:

Damage / Attack Speed

Health / Armor

Spell Power / Mana Regeneration

Healing / Crowd Control / Mana Regeneration

Movement Speed

Movement speed upgrades are extremely rare because they are powerful.

Upgrade process:

Only one player can upgrade one power at a time.

Upgrade duration:

Around 35 seconds.

---

# Blood Coin System

Hero kills reward Blood Coins.

Blood Coins are used at Armories.

Players upgrade their important items.

Starting items are weaker and cheaper to upgrade.

This gives PvP kills additional value.
