Aetherfall is a fantasy PvPvE hero game inspired by the charm, hero control and exploration of Warcraft 3, but designed as its own modern game rather than a traditional MOBA.

The goal is to create a large fantasy battlefield where players control a single powerful hero, explore the world, level up, kill creeps and bosses, collect equipment, mine resources and fight other players.

Skill should always be the most important factor, while random loot, world events and exploration make every match different.

## Core Vision

Aetherfall should feel like playing a Warcraft 3 hero inside a much larger living world.

There are no traditional MOBA lanes and no worker/base-building gameplay.

Instead, the map is filled with creep camps, bosses, shops, mines, Blacksmiths, Goblin merchants, hidden locations, world events and other players.

The current long-term multiplayer idea is to have many small teams sharing the same world.

Examples:

20 players = 4 teams of 5.

40 players = 8 teams of 5.

Eventually, potentially 100 players = 20 teams of 5.

Solo, Duo and Trio modes could also exist later.

Players should be able to queue together with friends, with around 5 players being the normal maximum party size.

The goal is to make the world feel dangerous because there are always other teams somewhere on the map without making the individual player feel insignificant like they might in a 50v50 or 100v100 battle.

## Heroes

Every player controls one hero.

Heroes can reach a maximum level of 25 and have their own abilities, Ultimate, summon ability, stats, equipment and playstyle.

The initial class families are:

Slayer / Marksman - Rage

Defender / Giant - Protection

Wizard / Necromancer - Intelligence

Priest / Paladin - Restoration

The long-term goal is to have a very large number of unique heroes within these classes.

Two heroes belonging to the same class should still be able to play completely differently.

At level 25, a hero becomes the Master version of their class, such as Master Slayer.

The Master transformation gives the hero a Legendary Cloak, improves their abilities slightly and currently includes a short-range Teleport ability with a long cooldown.

## Player Identity

Every player should have their own strong, recognizable color similar to Warcraft 3.

The player's color can appear through their ground circle, aura, equipment accents, summons and some spell effects.

There should eventually be a large palette of bright colors so individual players become recognizable during a match.

Player color represents the individual player, not whether they are friendly or hostile.

Enemies are instead immediately recognizable through a red health bar.

The goal is to keep battles readable even when many differently colored heroes, summons and spells are on screen.

## Creep Camps

Creep camps are one of the most important parts of Aetherfall.

They are the primary source of:

Experience

Gold

Items

Potions

Camps have different difficulties:

Easy

Normal

Hard

Extreme

Harder camps provide more experience and gold and have better chances of dropping powerful equipment.

The map should contain a lot of creep camps so exploring and deciding which camps to fight becomes an important part of every match.

## Bosses

Bosses are a major part of Aetherfall.

The hardest bosses should be genuinely difficult encounters that cannot simply be soloed by a powerful hero.

Boss fights should require skill and teamwork.

Boss mechanics can include:

Dodging attacks.

Interrupting abilities.

Managing aggro.

Protecting teammates.

Destroying summoned enemies.

Multiple phases.

Positioning correctly.

Avoiding dangerous areas.

World bosses should be some of the hardest content in the game.

The strongest normal equipment should primarily come from defeating the hardest bosses rather than simply purchasing it from shops.

This makes powerful equipment something players earn by taking risks.

## Items

Heroes have a large equipment/inventory system, currently planned at approximately 16 normal item slots plus around 4 dedicated potion slots.

Items can include:

Weapons.

Armor.

Shields.

Auras.

Stat items.

Permanent-effect items.

Consumables.

Rare artifacts.

Potions stack up to approximately 3 and have their own inventory slots.

Random drops are important because they make matches unpredictable, but RNG should never be stronger than player skill.

## Mining and Resources

Because Aetherfall has no workers, every hero carries a basic Pickaxe.

Ore deposits can randomly appear throughout the map.

Mining is secondary progression and should never become more profitable than actually fighting creeps and exploring the world.

Mined resources are stored separately from normal equipment.

Resources can stack in large quantities and are not lost when the hero dies.

Resources can be spent at different locations around the map.

Possible uses include:

Consumables.

Utility items.

Small permanent stat upgrades.

For example:

+1 Damage.

+1 Defense.

+1 Spell Power.

These permanent upgrades should be expensive.

Mining should provide slow, reliable progression, while the biggest increases in power come from difficult PvE, PvP and exploration.

## Aether Ore

Aether Ore is extremely rare and one of the most valuable resources in the game.

It can potentially come from:

Extremely rare Aether Veins.

World events.

Extreme creep camps at a very low chance.

Extremely difficult bosses.

Hidden locations.

Aether Ore is not simply used to buy equipment.

Its main purpose is Legendary Ascension.

## Blood Coins

Killing enemy heroes rewards Blood Coins.

Blood Coins are a special PvP resource.

They can be used to upgrade equipment and are also required for the most powerful progression system in the game.

This connects PvP with PvE progression.

## Legendary Ascension

The current plan is:

Powerful Boss Item + Aether Ore + Blood Coins = Legendary Item

A Legendary Ascension should not simply add more damage.

The item transforms.

It can receive:

A new name.

New appearance.

Powerful glow/effects.

Improved stats.

A unique Legendary ability or effect.

For example:

A difficult world boss drops an extremely powerful Epic sword.

The player later discovers Aether Ore.

They also earn enough Blood Coins by defeating other heroes.

They take everything to an Aether Forge and transform the sword into its Legendary version.

Legendary equipment should be genuinely rare.

A normal level 25 hero should NOT automatically have an inventory filled with Legendary items.

Seeing another player carrying an Aetherforged boss weapon should immediately tell you that this player has achieved something significant during the match.

## Shops and World Locations

The map should contain many useful locations rather than one central shop.

Possible locations include:

Goblin Merchants.

Blacksmiths.

Armories.

Aether Forges.

Magic Shops.

Alchemists.

Markets.

Enchanters.

Secret Merchants.

Travelling Merchants.

Mercenary Camps.

Some locations should be dangerous or contested.

For example, there may only be a few Aether Forges on a huge map.

A player carrying Aether Ore may therefore have to travel through dangerous territory to create their Legendary item.

The world itself should naturally create PvP encounters instead of constantly forcing players into artificial capture zones.

## Blacksmiths

Blacksmiths allow players to improve their heroes and equipment.

Possible upgrades include:

Damage / Attack Speed.

Health / Armor.

Spell Power / Mana Regeneration.

Healing / Crowd Control / Mana Regeneration.

Movement Speed.

Some upgrades can take time to complete, creating opportunities for other players to attack.

Movement-speed upgrades should be especially rare and expensive because movement is extremely powerful.

## Mercenaries and Summons

Aetherfall should remain hero-focused.

Players should NOT control large armies.

However, there may eventually be weak temporary followers available from Mercenary Camps.

Examples could include:

Goblin Bodyguards.

Medics.

Scouts.

Utility creatures.

These units should mostly control themselves and follow the hero.

Certain heroes, such as Necromancers or future Beastmasters, can have stronger summon mechanics because summoning is part of their hero identity.

The player should always feel like they are controlling their hero rather than playing an RTS.

## World Events

The world should change during the match.

Possible events include:

Aether Veins appearing.

Legendary bosses awakening.

Rare merchants arriving.

Special areas opening.

World bosses spawning.

An example announcement could be:

"The Worldbreaker has awakened."

These events naturally attract multiple teams to the same location.

One team may attack the boss.

Another may wait to ambush them.

Several teams may temporarily cooperate against the boss before fighting over the reward.

These unpredictable situations are an important part of Aetherfall.

## Economy Philosophy

Different activities should provide different types of progression.

Creep Camps:

Main source of XP, Gold and normal items.

Mining:

Resources, consumables and expensive small permanent stat increases.

PvP:

Blood Coins and other rewards.

Hard / Extreme Camps:

Powerful equipment.

Bosses:

Some of the best equipment.

World Bosses:

Extremely powerful and rare equipment.

Aether:

Legendary Ascension.

The strongest player should therefore need to interact with several parts of the game rather than simply farming one activity repeatedly.

## Art Direction

The primary visual inspiration is Warcraft 3.

Aetherfall should NOT be photorealistic.

The goal is:

Charming stylized fantasy.

Strong colors.

Recognizable hero silhouettes.

Large readable weapons and armor.

Individual glowing player colors.

Readable spell effects.

Modern lighting, animation and effects.

The idea is essentially to preserve the charm and readability of Warcraft 3 while making it look like a modern game.

## Game Engine

Current planned engine:

Unity 6.

Renderer:

Universal Render Pipeline (URP).

Programming language:

C#.

Unity was chosen because it is suitable for stylized 3D games, works well for solo development and allows the project to be built incrementally.

Unity 6 is currently being installed.

## Development Strategy

Aetherfall is being developed as a solo project with heavy AI assistance.

ChatGPT will initially be used for:

Game design.

Architecture.

C# programming.

Debugging.

Planning.

Documentation.

Balancing.

Ideas.

As the codebase becomes larger, a dedicated AI coding agent such as Cursor or GitHub Copilot may be added.

The goal is to use AI heavily while still understanding and controlling the architecture and design of the game.

Strong individual player color.

Health and mana.

Basic attack.

One creep.

Red enemy health bar.

Creep death.

XP.

Gold.

Leveling.

One ability.

One item drop.

One potion drop.

Basic inventory.

Once this works, more systems can gradually be added.

## Multiplayer Development

The long-term vision may involve 20-100 players in a match.

However, development will begin much smaller.

First:

Single-player gameplay prototype.

Then:

2-player networking test.

Then:

Small multiplayer tests.

Then:

10-20 player testing.

Only after the architecture and performance have been proven should development move toward very large player counts.

The systems should still be designed with future multiplayer in mind from the beginning.

## Major Unresolved Questions

The biggest unanswered question is currently the exact win condition.

Ideas discussed include:

Last team standing.

Ancient Ritual.

World objectives.

Relic control.

Boss-related objectives.

Combinations of multiple systems.

Other major questions include:

How death and respawning work.

How long a match lasts.

How players spawn.

How teams are distributed around the world.

How the game prevents teams from simply hiding forever.

How the map gradually brings players into conflict.

How large the first real multiplayer mode should be.

How matchmaking works.

How much progression resets between matches.

These should be solved gradually rather than trying to design everything before testing the actual game.

## Long-Term Goal

Aetherfall should become a large fantasy PvPvE game where many small teams enter the same living world.

Players explore, level their heroes, hunt monsters, defeat extremely difficult bosses, discover rare resources, build powerful equipment and fight other teams.

The game should reward skill while still containing enough randomness and exploration that no two matches feel exactly the same.

The goal is not to make another MOBA.

The goal is to create something that captures the feeling of controlling a Warcraft 3 hero while turning that experience into a completely new modern multiplayer game.
